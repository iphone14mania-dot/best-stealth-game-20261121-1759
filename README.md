A top-down 2D stealth heist game: five museum wings, 25 patrolling guards with vision cones, 33 treasures and 5 target gems to steal, then reach the exit unseen. Hide in bushes and shadow, throw coins to distract guards, puff smoke to break sight — and ghost a level for the bonus.

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve `game/` from any static server). No network, no build step.

- **Move** WASD / arrows · **Sneak** Shift · **Run** Space
- **Coin** Q or click a tile · **Smoke** E · **Pause** P / Esc · **Restart** R · **Mute** M

Hand it in: `node main.ts` (writes `game/`, `screenshots/` and `dossier.json` to `/output`, or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
